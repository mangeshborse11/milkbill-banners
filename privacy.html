Milk Bill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · App: Milk Bill: Doodh Hisab & Dairy (com.milkbill.doodhhisab)

Milk Bill helps dairy owners and milk sellers keep daily milk records, prepare monthly bills and track payments. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information you enter

You may enter your dairy name, your name, mobile number, address, UPI ID, GSTIN and logo, and details of your customers (names, mobile numbers, areas, milk quantities, rates, payments), farmers, cattle and expenses. This information is used only to run the app's features for you, such as calculating bills and preparing messages.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: all data is saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Online backup (only if you log in): when you log in with Google or your mobile number, your data is copied to your private account on our cloud database (Supabase) so you can restore it on a new phone. Database security rules allow only your logged-in account to read or change your data. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Daily online backups of your data are kept for up to 30 days so mistakes can be undone.

3. Login information

If you choose Google login, we receive your Google account email address and basic profile from Google. If you choose mobile login, your mobile number is used to send a one-time password (OTP) through an SMS provider. This information is used only to identify your account.

4. Messages and bills

When you send a bill, receipt or reminder, the app opens WhatsApp, SMS or another app you choose, with the message ready. The message is sent by you through that app; Milk Bill does not send messages on its own and does not read your chats.

5. Payments

  • Premium / Business plans are purchased through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we do not receive your card, UPI or bank details. We store your plan status and a Google purchase reference to unlock features.
  • UPI QR codes on bills contain your own UPI ID so your customers can pay you directly. Milk Bill does not process or hold these payments.

6. Banners

The app downloads promotional banner images from our website. No personal data is sent when banners are downloaded.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data or your customers' data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising based on your data.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos or location. If you choose a logo, only that one image is used.

8. Your choices and deleting data

  • You can use the app without logging in; your data then stays only on your phone.
  • You can delete your account and all online data from inside the app: More → Cloud backup → Delete my account and online data. This removes your account, your online data and your online backups permanently.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.
  • You can also request deletion by contacting us (see below).

9. Children

Milk Bill is a business tool meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact
