Milk Bill is an app for keeping dairy milk records, preparing bills and tracking payments. By using the app you agree to these terms.

2. Your data and your customers

You are responsible for the information you enter, including your customers' names and phone numbers, and for having their permission to send them bills and reminders. Please check bills before sending them; calculations are based on the entries and rates you provide.

3. Plans and payments

  • The Free plan can be used without payment, for up to 5 customers and 5 animals, with no time limit. Premium allows up to 100 customers and 25 animals; Business has no limit.
  • Premium and Business plans are monthly subscriptions bought through Google Play, at the price shown in the app before you buy. They renew automatically every month until you cancel in the Play Store (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • If a subscription ends, the app returns to the Free plan. Your data is kept.

4. Payments to you

UPI QR codes in the app contain your own UPI ID. Payments from your customers go directly to your bank account through their UPI app. Milk Bill does not collect, hold or guarantee these payments.
